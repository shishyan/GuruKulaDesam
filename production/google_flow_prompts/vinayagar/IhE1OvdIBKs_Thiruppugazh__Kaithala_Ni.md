> *O Dark-visaged Elephant God who delights in offerings of palm-filling fruits (mango, banana, jackfruit), sweet appams, aval, and puffed rice! O dweller in the intellect of sincere seekers, O wish-yielding Kalpavriksha tree, O ambrosial honey of divine grace! Son of Lord Shiva crowned with the crescent moon and datura flower, endowed with mighty heroic shoulders, majestic round pot-belly, beloved son of Mother Parvati—I surrender and worship Your lotus feet with fresh blossoms!*
