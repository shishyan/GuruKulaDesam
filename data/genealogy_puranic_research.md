# Comprehensive Puranic and Itihasic Genealogy (Vamsanucharita)
*Synthesis across the 12 Puranas and Itihasas (Vishnu, Bhagavata, Matsya, Vayu, Brahma, Brahmanda, Harivamsa, Mahabharata, Valmiki Ramayana, Linga, Garuda, Markandeya)*

---

## Executive Overview
In classical Puranic literature, **Vamsanucharita** (dynastic genealogy) is celebrated as one of the essential **Pancha Lakshana** (five core characteristics) defining an authentic Mahapurana:
> *सर्गश्च प्रतिसर्गश्च वंशो मन्वन्तराणि च। वंश्यानुचरितं चेति पुराणं पञ्चलक्षणम्॥*  
> *(Sargascha pratisargascha vamsho manvantarani cha | Vamsyanucharitam cheti puranam panchalakshanam ||)*

This research report documents the unbroken ancestral line spanning from the Cosmic Dawn (Narayana/Brahma) through the Prajapatis, the 14 Manus, and the two cardinal royal dynasties of ancient Bharatavarsha:
1. **Surya Vamsha (The Solar Dynasty / Ikshvaku Dynasty)** ruling from Ayodhya (Kosala).
2. **Chandra Vamsha (The Lunar Dynasty / Aila Dynasty)** ruling from Pratishthana, Hastinapura, and Mathura.

## 1. Cosmic Origin: Brahma to the Manasaputras & Svayambhuva Manu

The primordial cosmic manifestation proceeds from the unmanifest supreme Paramatman (Narayana) to Hiranyagarbha Brahma:
- **Narayana (Parabrahman) -> Brahma (Svayambhu)**

### Svayambhuva Manu and Queen Shatarupa
Brahma bifurcated his divine form into the first sovereign male, **Svayambhuva Manu**, and the first female, **Queen Shatarupa**:
- **Priyavrata**: Ruled the earth; the tracks of his celestial chariot created the seven oceans (*Sapta-samudras*) and seven cosmic continents (*Sapta-dvipas*). Ancestor of Maharaja Nabhi, Rishabhadeva (Vishnu Avatara), and Emperor **Bharata** (after whom *Bharatavarsha* was first named).
- **Uttanapada**: Father of the exemplary child-devotee **Dhruva Maharaja** (the Pole Star Sovereign) by Queen Suniti. Dhruva's line led to King Vena and the glorious **Maharaja Prithu** (Prithu Vainya, milker of the earth, after whom the earth is termed *Prithvi*).
- **Three Daughters**:
  1. *Akuti*: Married Prajapati Ruchi; mother of **Yajna Avatara** and Dakshina.
  2. *Devahuti*: Married Prajapati Kardama; mother of **Bhagavan Kapila Muni** (founder of Sankhya).
  3. *Prasuti*: Married Prajapati Daksha; mother of 24 daughters including Sati Devi.

### The Ten Offspring of Vaivasvata Manu
1. **Ikshvaku**: Eldest son; establisher of the Solar Dynasty (*Surya Vamsha*) in Ayodhya.
2. **Nabhaga (Nriga)**: Renowned for unbounded cattle donations; temporarily turned into a chameleon due to an unintentional brahmin cow conflict; liberated by Sri Krishna (*Bhagavata* 10.64).
3. **Dhrishta**: Progenitor of martial rulers who embraced brahminical status (*Dharshthas*).
4. **Saryati**: Father of Sukanya (wife of Sage Chyavana) and Anarta (namesake of Gujarat/Anarta; capital Kushasthali).
5. **Narishyanta**: Ancestor of the northern martial races (*Sakas*).
6. **Pramshu**: Lineage produced righteous kings celebrated in the *Markandeya Purana*.
7. **Nabhagadishta (Dista)**: Line of virtuous vaishya-protecting monarchs (Karandhama, Avikshit, and the great Emperor Marutta).
8. **Karusha**: Progenitor of the valiant Karusha warrior communities.
9. **Prishadhra**: Inadvertently killed his guru's cow in the nocturnal dark; accepted his guru's curse with equanimity and achieved liberation through rigorous forest asceticism.
10. **Ila / Sudyumna**: Transformed alternately between male (Sudyumna) and female (Ila) by the enchantments of Shiva-Parvati's grove. As **Ila**, married Budha to give birth to Pururavas (founding the **Lunar Dynasty**); as Sudyumna, fathered Utkala, Gaya, and Vinatashva.

### B. Chandra Vamsha (The Lunar Dynasty / Aila Dynasty)

```
                       BRAHMA
                          |
                        ATRI (m. Anasuya)
                          |
                    CHANDRA (Soma) (m. Tara)
                          |
                    BUDHA (Mercury) (m. Ila, daughter of Vaivasvata Manu)
                          |
                    PURURAVAS (Aila) (m. Urvashi Apsara)
                          |
                 +--------+--------+
                 |                 |
                AYU             AMAVASU (Kanauj line; Jahnu, Vishvamitra)
                 |
              NAHUSHA (Temporarily Indra; fell as Python; redeemed by Yudhishthira)
                 |
              YAYATI (Exchanged old age for youth with son Puru)
                 |
     +-----------+-----------+---------------+---------------+
     |                       |               |               |
   YADU                    PURU           TURVASU         DRUHYU           ANU
(m. Devayani)         (m. Sharmishtha) (m. Devayani)   (m. Sharmishtha) (m. Sharmishtha)
Yadavas, Haihayas     Pauravas, Kurus   Yavanas/South   Gandharas/North  Anga, Vanga, Kalinga
Vrishnis (KRISHNA)    Pandavas/Kauravas
```

#### The Five Royal Branches of Emperor Yayati:

#### 1. Yadu (The Yadava House)
- Exiled to southwestern territories (Mathura, Surasena, Saurashtra, Anarta, Narmada).
- **Two Major Sub-branches**:
  1. *Haihayas*: From Sahasrajit; produced **Mahishmat** (founder of Mahishmati) and **Kartavirya Arjuna (Sahasrabahu Arjuna)**, who subdued Ravana, possessed 1,000 arms, and was eventually slain by Parashurama.
  2. *Kroshtu Line (Vrishnis and Andhakas)*:
     - *Kroshtu -> Vrijinivat -> Shashabindu (Chakravartin) -> Vidarbha (founded Vidarbha kingdom) -> Satvata (founder of Satvatas)*.
     - Satvata's sons: **Andhaka** (father of Ahuka -> Ugrasena & Devaka; maternal grandfather of Krishna) and **Vrishni**.
     - Vrishni -> Devamidhusha -> **Shurasena** (father of Vasudeva and Kunti/Pritha).
     - **VASUDEVA (Anakadundubhi)** married Devaki.
     - **BHAGAVAN SRI KRISHNA (8th Avatara)** & **BALARAMA (Adi Shesha Avatara)**.
     - Sri Krishna & Rukmini -> **Pradyumna** (Kama Avatara) -> **Aniruddha** (m. Usha) -> **Vajra** (Sole survivor; coronated King of Mathura/Indraprastha by the Pandavas).

#### 2. Puru (The Paurava & Kuru House)
- Youngest son of Yayati; happily offered his youth to his aged father and was awarded the prime ancestral empire of **Pratishthana / Madhyadesha**.
- *Puru -> Janamejaya I -> Prachinvat -> Manasyu -> Dushyanta (m. Shakuntala)*.
- **EMPEROR BHARATA (Sarvadamana)**: Son of Dushyanta and Shakuntala; performed 400 major Ashvamedha sacrifices; established the foundational pan-Indian identity **BHARATAVARSHA**.
- *Bharata -> Bhumanyu -> Suhotra -> Hastin (Founded the imperial capital **HASTINAPURA**)*.
- *Hastin -> Ajamidha -> Riksha -> Samvarana (m. Tapati, daughter of the Sun God)*.
- **KING KURU**: Consecrated the sacred soil of **KURUKSHETRA** through supreme penance and vows; his scions were crowned *Kauravas*.
- *Kuru -> Pratipa -> MAHARAJA SHANTANU*.
  - Shantanu & Ganga -> **BHISHMA (Devavrata)** (Took the lifelong vow of celibacy).
  - Shantanu & Satyavati -> Chitrangada and Vichitravirya.
  - Sage **Veda Vyasa** (son of Satyavati & Sage Parashara) blessed the queens through Niyoga:
    - Queen Ambika -> **Dhritarashtra** (Father of Duryodhana and the 100 Kauravas).
    - Queen Ambalika -> **Pandu** (Father of the 5 Pandavas: Yudhishthira, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadeva).
    - Parishrami Maid -> **Vidura** (Incarnation of Dharma).
- **Arjuna** & Subhadra -> **Abhimanyu** (Hero of Kurukshetra War; slain inside the Chakravyuha).
- Abhimanyu & Uttara -> **PARIKSHIT II**: Preserved by Sri Krishna inside the womb from Ashvatthama's Brahmashira weapon; crowned Emperor of the Earth; heard the *Srimad Bhagavatam* from Suka Maharshi.
- Parikshit -> **JANAMEJAYA II**: Performed the Snake Sacrifice (*Sarpa Satra*); recipient of the *Mahabharata* recitation by Sage Vaishampayana.
- *Janamejaya II -> Shatanika -> Ashvamedhadatta -> Nichakshu* (transferred the capital from flood-ravaged Hastinapura to **Kausambi**).
- Down through 25 generations to **Kshemaka** (the last celebrated Puru sovereign in Kali Yuga).

#### 3. Turvasu
- Assigned the southeastern frontier; associated in Harivamsa and Matsya Purana with early southern kingdoms (Cholas, Pandyas, Keralas); later his royal line merged into the Pauravas through adoption by King Marutta.

#### 4. Druhyu
- Settled the northwestern frontier (Gandhara, named after King Gandhara; Aratta); expanded trans-Indus into Central/Western Asia.

#### 5. Anu
- Reigned over eastern realms; produced King Ushinara (father of the righteous **Shibi Chakravartin**) and King Titikshu, whose descendants founded the five celebrated eastern realms: **Anga, Vanga, Kalinga, Pundra, and Suhma**.

## 6. Summary of Dynastic Confluences

1. **Unity of Source**: Both the Solar and Lunar dynasties converge at Lord Brahma — Surya Vamsha descending through **Marichi -> Kashyapa -> Vivasvan -> Vaivasvata Manu**, and Chandra Vamsha descending through **Atri -> Chandra -> Budha** and uniting with Vaivasvata Manu's daughter **Ila**.
2. **Prophetic Continuation**: The Puranas explicitly predict that both lineages remain spiritually preserved in the hidden Himalayan sanctuary of **Kalapa**:
   - **King Maru** of the Solar dynasty (*Bhagavata* 9.12.6) and **King Devapi** of the Lunar dynasty (*Bhagavata* 9.22.17) will return at the conclusion of Kali Yuga to establish the righteous royal dynasties of the next Satya Yuga.
